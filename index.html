01 / Synthesis

Scene builder

Choose aircraft and set their paths. Preset timbre is procedural and still needs comparison with field recordings.

Country selects the first aircraft and the default for new aircraft. Set equal closest-approach offsets for simultaneous passes. The mix starts before the first pass so you can hear the approach.

Left and right offset are relative to the direction of travel. The stereo image is relative to where the microphone faces. A 90° track with the mic facing north travels left to right.
